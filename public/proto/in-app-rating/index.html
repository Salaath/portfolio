In-app rating

Clickable prototype of the new in-app rating sheet. The stars run the Rive file, and taps go straight to its state machine.

Trigger point

Flows to try

  • 1–3 stars"What went wrong?" chips, then a thank-you with a green check.
  • 4–5 starsAs a first rating: no chips, the store prompt opens once the star animation plays. After a 1–3 rating: you stay on the sheet with "What went well?" chips, and Submit opens the store prompt.
  • Other chipOpens the feedback field already focused, with the keyboard up.
  • DismissCancel, Maybe later, tap the dimmed area, or drag the handle down.
